# B3: build note for the 622 numerics in Lean

The tier L = 622 uses the same pipeline as the 711 tier (`B2.md`), with M = 7, 8 chain vectors over j < 7 and one weight vector
(`weights_622.json`, integers over 2^50). The statement and the proof outline are in `README.md` (step B3).

## Design

`CombNumerics` is the chain statement: `N(v) < 2 w_1 S cos(theta_1/2)` for each `v` in `V`, and `V` contains `cvec(P1, u)` for every `u` in `[0,1)`.
It does not depend on a cell cover of `u`: the weight vector is one vector for all cells, and the ambiguous-floor cases are exactly the chain breakpoints,
covered by `chain_mem` (a case split on `u` against 7 thresholds, 8 leaves). The closed form fails for 622 (R = 1.0179), but the chain check passes,
checked in exact integer arithmetic over all 2000 zeros (`gen622.py --full`, 4 min): the largest ratio per chain vector is 0.9833, a margin of 1.7 percent.
The smallest chain gap is 0.044, so the precision needed for `P1` is loose. `7 * period <= 622` follows from `P_bounds` (`P <= 88.848139`); `zero1_facts` and the
tail bound (`Wtail_le`, `Cconst_le`) are reused from the 711 tier.

## Files, all under `lean/`

* `gen622.py`: `gen711.py` with `cert_comb_622.json` and namespace `KeiperComb.N622`; options `--cert`, `--chunk CI K0 K1 OUT`, `--full`.
* `gen622_all.sh`: writes `Numerics622Cert.lean` and the chunks `Numerics622C00` to `Numerics622C18` (C00 = zeros 1..199, C01 = 200..399, C02 to C17 = 100 each, C18 = 1999).
* `gen622_chain.py`: writes `Numerics622Chain.lean` (`leaf0` to `leaf7`, `chain_mem` for j < 7).
* `KeiperComb/Numerics622AsmGen.lean` and `KeiperComb/Numerics622Assembly.lean`: the glue and the final inequality, transformed from the 711 versions
  (M = 7, 8 vectors; `combNumerics_622`, `theoremA'_622_numerics`, `theoremA'_622_runs_numerics`).
* `check_intervals.py`: checks that the paper's zero intervals lie inside the Lean intervals (radius 1025 units of 2^-30).

The chunk modules import `Numerics711Sound3` (the generic `checkBatchFast_sound`) and `Numerics622Cert`. None of the `Numerics622*` modules is imported
by `KeiperComb.lean`; the lake target `KeiperCombNumerics` builds them.

## How to build

```
LEAN_NUM_THREADS=1 lake build KeiperComb.Numerics622Assembly
```

Do not set `ulimit -v`. Build a chunk alone with `lake build KeiperComb.Numerics622C<CI>`.

## Checks

* Every chunk and the assembly: no `sorry`, no `native_decide`. `#print axioms` of `N622.intervalsOrdered`, `N622.theoremA'_622_numerics` and
  `N622.theoremA'_622_runs_numerics`: `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` only. Logs with the axiom lines: `logs/fidelity_r1_*.log`.
* `NegTestsNumerics.lean` (red-first checks): 621 is refused, the checker refuses `U = 0` and `rho = 0`, and `final_nat` fails with a tenfold tail bound.
* A coverage note: C02 is used whole (C01 ends at zero 399 and C02 starts at zero 400); dropping a zero from the list makes the length 1998 and the kernel check fails.
