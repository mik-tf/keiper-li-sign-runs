## Findings that change the brief
1. The "closed form" `R_M(a)` of `cert_comb_711.json` uses `|A(alpha_k)|` with `alpha_k = 2 pi theta_k / theta_1`, a transcendental angle. It does not reduce to rational q_k powers. The B1 statement `CombNumerics` is instead about the chain `V` of M+1 = 9 integer vectors, `N(v) < 2 w_1 S cos(theta_1/2)`, and there `e^{i v theta_k} = q_k^v` is exactly a power of a rational Gaussian number. So the spike follows the S1 chain checker, not the closed form.
2. Zero midpoints in `z2000.json` have about 200 bits. Plan: round to the grid 2^-30 and widen the interval to half-width 2^-20 + 2^-31 (`H = 2^10+1` grid units); a superset of the certified interval, so `ZeroIntervals` stays true.
3. LeanCert is not needed: no arctan or cos of a transcendental argument is required (see route).
4. Exact integer arithmetic beats interval arithmetic here: with `q = Z/D`, `Z = (N^2 - 2^58) + i N 2^30`, `D = N^2 + 2^58`, `2^50 D^vm B_m = sum A_j D^(vm-v_j) Z^(v_j)` is a Gaussian integer X + iY (GMP-accelerated in the kernel, `decide +kernel`, no `native_decide`). The sqrt is a witness `rho`, checked as `2^80 (X^2+Y^2) <= rho^2 (2^50 D^vm)^2`.
